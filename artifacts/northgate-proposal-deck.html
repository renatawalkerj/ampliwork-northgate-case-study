Part 1 · The Pitch — 3 / 8

What we propose: one agent, a deterministic pipeline around it, a human at the end of every path

1. Ingest & check

Read SAP + documents. Entity conflicts and compliance gaps are caught by plain code — no model needed, no model risk.

2. The one agent

A single reasoning model determines charged-correctly / recoverable / where-reported, with evidence and a confidence score. Not several agents stitched together.

3. Route

High confidence → batch-confirm. Low confidence → full review, evidence attached. Never write-back — a hard constraint, not a design choice.

4. File & learn

The analyst files it herself, always. Her overrides become reviewed, auditable knowledge-base rules — not judgment lost after the fact.

Where the human stays, and why: the system cannot write to SAP. Every determination needs an analyst action before filing, at every confidence level — a constraint, not a choice.

Part 1 · The Pitch — 4 / 8

The information model — eight entities, one flow

Legal entity
The Northgate operating company that books the transaction and files the return.
Jurisdiction
The actual rules boundary — not hub. Its own reclaim window, invoice rules, filing frequency.
Supplier
The real-world business Northgate buys from — resolved across possibly-duplicate vendor records.
Transaction
The atomic unit of record. One supplier, one invoice, one legal entity, one point in time.
Invoice
The supplier's own document — not always sufficient alone for 2 of the 3 required determinations.
Recoverability determination
The output: charged correctly / recoverable / where reported, plus confidence.
Filing period
The monthly or quarterly cadence a determination gets swept into.
Reclaim
The actual right to recover input tax — alive until its jurisdiction's window closes.
A legal entity buys from a supplier (resolved past duplicate vendor records) → a transaction, documented by an invoice → evaluated against its jurisdiction's rules → a recoverability determination → filed in the next filing period → if recoverable, an active reclaim, ticking down to its jurisdiction's window.

Left out of v1: legal entity isn't yet independent from jurisdiction (assumed near 1:1); credit notes are named but not yet linked to a specific prior transaction; filing period is a jurisdiction attribute, not its own tracked record. Also out: automated supplier outreach, full China fapiao support, SAP consolidation.

What breaks if this is wrong: a hub-level model would treat a 5-year Netherlands claim as expired at the coarser EMEA minimum — actively abandoning money that's still recoverable.

Part 1 · The Pitch — 6 / 8

The risk, and how we bound it

For "is this transaction recoverable":

True positive

Flagged recoverable, genuinely is.

False positive

Flagged recoverable, isn't — the $4M penalty problem.

False negative

Not flagged, was recoverable — part of the $8–30M left on the table.

True negative

Not flagged, genuinely not recoverable.

  • High confidence → batch-confirm. Low → full review. Both still end with an analyst — "slow is better than wrong" at every tier.
  • Precision on auto-processed, recall on routing. Phase 1 measures the human-only baseline the system has to beat.
  • Overrides feed the knowledge base, reviewed before they count — the false-positive rate is designed to fall over time, not stay fixed.

We don't ask her to double-check what she's told is safe — we build the system to earn not needing that.

Part 1 · The Pitch — 7 / 8

Phasing, timeline, pricing logic

  • The 11-week security review runs in parallel with the 8-week sandboxed build — the only way the year-end-close ask is achievable at all.
  • Phase 1 (EMEA/Rotterdam): clears the urgent-recoverables backlog. Cash first, not headcount — doesn't depend on the coding-bottleneck diagnosis being right.
  • Phase 2+: India, targeted — not "all of APAC." Cost-of-Delay ranked, not just build-tractability ranked.
  • Pricing: fixed-fee + narrow contingency in Phase 1, expanding once 3 conditions are proven — human-in-the-loop control, tracked metrics, below-baseline error cost.
