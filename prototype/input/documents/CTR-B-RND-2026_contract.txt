AGREEMENT — Reinraum Technik GmbH and Northgate Industries GmbH (Entity B, Germany)
Effective: 2026-03-01

Scope of engagement: Supplier shall provide specialized cleanroom-classified tooling and
fittings for the Northgate R&D Facility, Building B, including the ISO Class 5 laboratory
and manufacturing floor currently under fit-out for the Advanced Materials research program.

Cost allocation: All charges under this agreement are to be booked against cost center
CC-4520-RND (R&D Facility, Building B) and are not general facilities or office-equipment
spend.

This is a standalone third-party commercial supply agreement. Reinraum Technik GmbH is not
an affiliated entity, subsidiary, or intra-group counterparty of Northgate Industries.
