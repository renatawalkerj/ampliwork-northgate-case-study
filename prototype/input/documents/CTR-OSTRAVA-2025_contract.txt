SERVICE AGREEMENT — Ostrava Precision Tooling s.r.o. (Czech Republic) and Northgate
Industries S.A.S. (France)
Effective: 2025-11-01

Scope: Precision tooling calibration and maintenance services, performed on Ostrava's own
premises and remotely, billed on a per-engagement basis to Northgate's French manufacturing
entity.

Relationship: Ostrava Precision Tooling s.r.o. is an independent third-party contractor.
This agreement does not create, and is not entered into pursuant to, any intra-group,
shared-services, or cost-sharing arrangement between Ostrava and any Northgate affiliate.
Payment terms are standard commercial terms (net 45).
